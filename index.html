The road brings us together

Different services.
Different stories.
One brotherhood.

We're the ACT chapter of the Veterans Motorcycle Club — military veterans and bikers bound by service, brotherhood, respect and remembrance.

Some of our stories begin in the Army, Navy or Air Force. Others are shaped by deployments, family sacrifice or the hard road of finding new purpose after Defence. No two are the same — but when the engines start, those differences matter less.

Like every VMC chapter across Australia, we carry forward one mission: to respond to and support veterans in times of need.

Service doesn't end when the uniform comes off.
What we do

More than a ride

Sometimes support starts with a coffee, a phone call or a quiet ride through the countryside. It doesn't need to be complicated — what matters is being present.

Mateship on the road

Social rides and chapter gatherings that give veterans space to think, reconnect and spend time with people who understand.

Veterans supporting veterans

Checking on a mate who's gone quiet, connecting people with professional help, and standing beside families in hard times.

Remembrance

Commemorative rides, memorial services and Anzac Day traditions that keep the names and stories of the fallen alive.

Community

Charity runs, fundraising and backing local causes — so when Canberra sees us on the road, they see veterans still serving.
