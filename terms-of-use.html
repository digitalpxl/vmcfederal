General information only

We work hard to keep the information on this site accurate and up to date, but it's provided for general information only.

In particular, the Veteran Support & Entitlements guide is a starting point to help you find services — it is not legal, financial, medical or professional advice, and it isn't an eligibility decision. Entitlements, rates and programs change. Always check the official source (such as DVA or Service NSW) or speak to a qualified advocate before relying on it.

In an emergency, call 000. For 24/7 veteran and family counselling, call Open Arms on 1800 011 046.

Events & rides

When you register for a chapter event, such as the Tour of Duty Poker Run, the specific rules for that event (published with the event details) also apply.

  • You must hold a current licence for the vehicle you ride, and your motorcycle must be registered and roadworthy.
  • You're responsible for obeying road rules, riding safely and within your ability, and for your own safety equipment.
  • Follow reasonable directions from event organisers and marshals. We may refuse entry to, or remove, anyone whose behaviour is unsafe or disrespectful.
  • No alcohol or drugs while riding.
  • Patch clubs attend by invitation only.
  • Events may change or be cancelled because of weather, safety or circumstances outside our control. If we cancel an event, we'll let registered riders know as soon as we can and explain the options for their registration.

Motorcycle riding carries inherent risks. To the extent permitted by law, you take part in rides and events at your own risk. Any event-specific requirements, such as sign-in on the day, will be explained when you register.
