Good to know

First run? No worries.

What is a poker run?

A social motorcycle ride with designated stops and a card-hand challenge. You collect cards along the way, and the best poker hand at the finish wins. We'll share the route and rules before the event.

Do I have to be a veteran?

No. Veterans, serving ADF members and the wider motorcycle community are all welcome. Patch clubs attend by invitation only.

What if I'm coming alone?

That's fine — plenty of people do. It's a great chance to meet other riders. Come and say g'day.

What bike do I need?

Whatever you ride. All makes and models are welcome — no Harley required.

Where do we finish?

The route is a mystery until the day — that's part of the fun. Start with a full tank; fuel is available along the way.

How do I stay updated?

Follow Veterans MC Federal Chapter on Facebook and Instagram for announcements, route news and photos.
