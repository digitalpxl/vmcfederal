Federal Chapter ride

The journey to Drouin

Members of the Federal Chapter set out on Friday 20 October and rode to Drouin, Victoria, for the dedication of the Gippsland Chapter Fallen Brothers Memorial Wall on Saturday 21 October.

Blessed with fine, sunny conditions, the ride carried a strong sense of purpose: a chance to stand beside fellow chapter members, support the Gippsland Chapter and honour the fallen brothers whose names are preserved on the wall.

A community effort

A year of work and commitment

Around a year of planning, construction and cooperation. Local people donated materials, VMC chapters supplied the details of fallen brothers, and the Gippsland Chapter funded and built it — uniting chapters, local veterans, ADF members and the wider Gippsland community.

An ongoing tribute

A continuing tradition of remembrance

The dedication is intended to become an annual event — a chance to honour more fallen brothers and for members, families and the community to come together.

The Memorial Wall will keep growing as a place of reflection, remembrance and connection between generations of veterans and their communities.

Lest we forget

Their service and brotherhood will be remembered. The Memorial Wall stands as a permanent reminder of service, sacrifice, friendship and the responsibility we share to remember those who have gone before us.
