Who we are

This website is run by Veterans MC Federal Chapter ("the chapter", "we", "us"), the Canberra-region chapter of the Veterans Motorcycle Club.

We respect your privacy. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), even where the Act may not strictly apply to a volunteer-run club of our size.

What we collect

We only collect information we need. Depending on how you deal with us, that may include:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), the type of enquiry and your message.
  • Event registrations and the supporters shop: your name, contact details and what you've registered for or ordered. Payments are handled by Square — we don't see or store your card details.
  • Membership enquiries: information you choose to share about your service history and why you'd like to join.
  • Social media: if you message, tag or comment on our Facebook or Instagram pages, we'll see whatever those platforms show us.
  • Photos and video taken at chapter events (see Photos at events).
  • Technical information: like most websites, our host and the services we embed may record your IP address, browser type and pages visited. If we use website analytics, it's only to understand how the site is used overall — never to identify you.

You can contact us without giving your full name, but we may not be able to help as well — for example, we can't reply without a way to reach you.

Sensitive information

We don't ask for sensitive information such as health, mental health, financial or identification details, and our contact form asks you not to include it.

If you choose to tell us something sensitive — for instance when reaching out for veteran support — we'll treat it in confidence and use it only to help you. We won't pass it on without your consent, unless the law requires it or we reasonably believe it's needed to lessen a serious threat to someone's life, health or safety.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000. For 24/7 veteran and family counselling, call Open Arms on 1800 011 046.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and keep in touch about it.
  • To run events such as the Tour of Duty Poker Run — registrations, safety briefings, results and prizes.
  • To process supporters-shop orders.
  • To respond to membership, sponsorship and community enquiries.
  • To connect veterans and families with support, with their agreement.
  • To share news, photos and event updates.
  • To meet legal, insurance and safety obligations.

We'll only send you news or event updates if you've asked for them or would reasonably expect them, and you can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. We only share it when we need to:

  • Chapter volunteers who need it to handle your enquiry, registration or order.
  • Service providers who help us run the site and events, such as our website host, Square (payments and online store), email providers, and Google (maps and video).
  • Support services such as Open Arms or a Veteran & Family Hub — only with your consent, except in an emergency as described above.
  • Authorities where the law requires or allows it.

Photos at events

Photos and video are often taken at rides, commemorations and other chapter events, and may be published on this website and our social media.

If you'd rather not be photographed, let an organiser know on the day. If a photo of you has been published and you'd like it removed, contact us and we'll take it down as soon as we reasonably can.

Cookies & embedded content

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Where non-essential cookies are used, you can accept or decline them through the cookie banner, and change your mind at any time.

Some pages include content from other services, which may set their own cookies or receive your IP address when the page loads:

  • Google Maps — maps on the Poker Run, Coffee Bunker and Contact pages.
  • YouTube — videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and only load once you press play.
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces used on this site.
  • Square — when you follow a link to register or buy, Square's own privacy policy applies.
  • Facebook and Instagram — when you follow links to our social pages.

Overseas storage

Some of the services we use — including Google, Square and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) — may store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We choose reputable providers, but their own privacy policies apply to how they handle your information.

Security & how long we keep it

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access — for example, limiting access to the volunteers who need it and using password-protected accounts.

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law, then securely delete or de-identify it.

No website or email system is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive information through the contact form.

Access & correction

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us and we'll respond within a reasonable time — usually within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first. If we can't agree to a request, we'll explain why.

Complaints

If you're concerned about how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. We'll look into it and get back to you.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Young people

This website isn't aimed at children. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us personal information or registering for an event.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
