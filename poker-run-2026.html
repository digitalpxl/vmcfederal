Thank you for riding with us.

VMC Federal extends our sincere respect and appreciation to everyone who rolled out and stood alongside us for the 2026 Ride for Remembrance.

Every rider, every supporter and every person who took the time to be part of the day helped make it something truly special. It's more than just a ride — it's about honouring those who came before us, remembering those we've lost, and standing together for those who are still with us.

To our sponsors and supporters: we owe you a massive thank you. Your generosity, loyalty and unwavering support are the backbone of events like this. Whether your contribution was big, small or behind the scenes, it did not go unnoticed. Without people and businesses like you beside us, these events simply wouldn't be possible.

To everyone who organised, volunteered, lent a hand or just turned up to show support — you are what makes this community what it is.

We ride for those who no longer can. We remember those who served. And we honour those who never made it home.
Veterans MC Federal ChapterService. Brotherhood. Respect.
